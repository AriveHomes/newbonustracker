    Bonus eligibility rules

    These five requirements are fixed for the Arive Homes superintendent bonus program.

    1. Build timeDig to Certificate of Occupancy: 150 days or less for single-family homes; 205 days or less for townhomes, after approved outside-control delays.
    2. Final grade photosPhotos must be uploaded to Dropbox and verified in the review.
    3. 30-day punch listAll items must be complete within 30 calendar days of closing and verified by homeowner signature, or written management approval when a homeowner signature is not reasonably obtainable.
    4. Safety / SWPPP inspectionsRequired safety and SWPPP inspections must be completed and documented.
    5. End of Build ChecklistEvery end-of-build checklist item must be completed, including city sidewalk approval/sign-off or an approved documented exception.

    All five requirements must be complete for the full base bonus to be recommended.
