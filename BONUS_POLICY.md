# Arive Homes Superintendent Bonus Program

## Purpose

Provide a simple and fair home-by-home bonus program that rewards timely completion and verifies that the home is fully closed out.

## Eligibility requirements

All five requirements must be complete:

### 1. Build time

Adjusted build time must be **150 calendar days or less for single-family homes, or 205 calendar days or less for townhomes**.

**Adjusted build time = Gross build time - approved exception days**

Approved exception days must be documented in the delay log, marked outside the superintendent's control, and approved by management. Overlapping dates count once.

### 2. Final grade photos

The required final-grade photos remain in the normal Arive Homes Dropbox folder. The reviewer marks the requirement complete in the tracker after confirming the photos were uploaded. An optional verified date and Dropbox reference may be entered for the audit trail.

### 3. 30-day punch list

All 30-day punch list items must be complete. The reviewer should enter the completion date and any useful notes.

### 4. Safety / SWPPP inspections

Required safety and SWPPP inspections must be completed and documented. The reviewer may enter a verification date and inspection reference or note.

### 5. End of Build Checklist

Every item on the in-app End of Build Checklist must be completed before the checklist criterion passes. The checklist includes final-grade photos, downspout extensions, exhaust terminations, sewer video, HVAC function and installation checks, dishwasher/disposal checks, garage keypad, breakers, blower-box dust, final interior/exterior debris cleanup, and city sidewalk approval/sign-off (or a documented management-approved exception when a separate city sign-off is not available).

## Payout

When all five requirements are complete, the app recommends 100% of the base bonus. When any requirement is incomplete, the calculated recommendation is $0. Management may document a manual override in the approval section.

## Delay exceptions

Examples that may qualify when documented and approved include city or inspection delays, utility delays, company or customer changes, unusual weather, design or engineering changes, material allocation, and development or site conditions.

Trade coordination problems, missed inspections, rework, poor sequencing, and avoidable superintendent-controlled delays normally should not qualify.


### 30-day punch-list tracking

The due date is automatically calculated as 30 calendar days after the Closing Date. Certificate of Occupancy remains separate and is used for the Dig-to-CO build-time calculation. The completion checkbox and completion date remain the final record of whether the requirement was met.


## Fixed base bonus

The base superintendent bonus is **$350 per qualifying home** and is not adjustable in the tracker.
